# Overview

## Idea
A close friend (the **creator**) names a friend (the **subject**), picks their gender, and adds 2-8 potential partners. The contenders are automatically the opposite gender (subject female -> "guys", subject male -> "girls"). The creator votes, then shares the link. Every friend who opens it votes; **only after voting** do they see how everyone else voted. Votes can never be changed.

## Audience
Gen Z in India (and diaspora). Short flows, Hinglish copy, mobile-first, built to be screenshotted and posted to Stories.

## Seasonal hook
Karwa Chauth 2026 is Thursday 29 Oct 2026 (India), moonrise ~8:17 PM IST in Delhi. The default "vibe" is Karwa Chauth (moon/chhalni themed copy and the **Chand** card design). The homepage shows a live moonrise countdown. Vibes: Karwa Chauth, Shaadi vibes, Just for fun.

## Journeys
1. **Creator**: Home -> Start -> 3-step wizard (friend, contenders, own vote) -> poll page with share kit.
2. **Voter**: Link -> sealed vote screen -> confirm sheet ("final") -> unsealing animation -> results + card studio.
3. **Listed person**: Opt-out page or email.

## USPs
- Sealed results enforced server-side
- Final votes
- Privacy-first: cards rendered on-device, no accounts, auto-delete
- Cards: 4 designs x 3 animations x 2 sizes, PNG/GIF/video, native share sheet to Stories
