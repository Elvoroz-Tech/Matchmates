# Research notes

- Karwa Chauth 2026: Thursday 29 October 2026 in India; moonrise Delhi about 8:17 PM IST, other cities vary by 15-45 min (Mumbai ~9:00 PM, Bengaluru ~8:55 PM). The USA/Canada observe on 28 Oct. The countdown targets the Delhi moonrise and is configurable.
- Karwa Chauth is traditionally a fast by married women for their husbands, with the moon seen through a sieve (chhalni). Unmarried women and friends increasingly join in playfully, hence the "Karwa Chauth partner" framing. Copy stays light and respectful.
